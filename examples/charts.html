Eight chart types, all card-sized

Trends over time get a line. Hovering indexing recovery shows nine months of movement with a filled area and an endpoint dot, sized to sit inside the card without scrolling.

Discrete periods get bars instead, which is what traffic growth uses. A single percentage against a whole gets a donut, so SERP CTR reads as a ring with the figure beside it. Completion against a target gets a plain progress bar, as in schema coverage.

Composition gets a stacked bar with a legend: crawl budget splits five million pages across four templates. Ranked comparison gets horizontal bars, so protocol adoption sorts six protocols by relative usage and emphasises the leader.

Position within a range gets a dot on a track, which is how Core Web Vitals shows where a number sits between best and worst. Runs of pass and fail get win-loss ticks, so the deploy record reads as a sequence rather than a single average.

Charts compose with any card kind, not just statistics. The llms.txt verdict carries one under its reasoning, and plain terms like MCP carry none at all.
