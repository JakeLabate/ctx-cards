Why your PDP is leaking revenue

Most mid-market retailers obsess over AOV while ignoring the pages that produce it. A product detail page that loads slowly, fails Core Web Vitals, or ships without Product schema is invisible to exactly the surfaces that now drive discovery.

Start with the basics. Every variant needs a canonical tag or you will fragment signals across near-identical URLs. Faceted navigation on a PLP will burn crawl budget if it is left open. An out of stock item should keep its page rather than 404.

Then measure honestly. Track CTR and impressions separately, because a ranking gain that does not move clicks is not a win. Watch cart abandonment against CAC to see whether acquisition spend is reaching customers who actually convert.

The forward-looking piece is agentic commerce. UCP and MCP are how agents will discover and buy from your catalog, and structured data is what they read. GEO is not a separate discipline here, it is the same JSON-LD work with a wider audience.
